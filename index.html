Tonight's plan

Astronomical dark divided into 10-minute slots, every eligible target scored in every slot, the winners coalesced into blocks of at least 45 minutes, at most 3 targets — a switch costs a slew, a plate solve and an autofocus, so churn is a real cost. Only projects with at least 1 h on record are scheduled: below that the fractional formula always prefers whatever has least data (a 0.88 h toehold took the whole night in testing), so the toehold tier is ranked separately, exactly as it is in the table below. Frame counts are the REALISTIC figure: the MEASURED 61 s median cadence (four nights, 679 subs) at 82% duty for dither, autofocus and plate solves — 74 s per frame — less 18 min for any meridian flip inside a block. Existing hours are discounted by how well they merge with tonight's settings, and count only if they were shot from Haxtun — another sky is never stacked with this one and counts as 0 h. Tonight's settings are gain 100 / 120 s, read from the 400 most recent frames on the drive. Full credit at the same gain and exposure; 93% at another gain (an estimate from one NGC 7331 experiment, good to a few percent at best); 93% at another exposure (NOT measured on Haxtun data — borrowed from the gain figure). Focal length is not a cohort key at all — measured 2026-10-02, Siril solves a 3% scale change from the stars alone.
